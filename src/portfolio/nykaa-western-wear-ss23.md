subtitle: This project showcases the Nykaa SS23 "Awestruck" collection,
  highlighting the complete fashion design process of developing a vibrant
  women's apparel line from initial concept to detailed technical specification.
  The portfolio features industry-standard rendered linesheets for contemporary
  garments, including strappy crop tops, off-shoulder cut-out tops, and finely
  ruched short dresses. Each technical CAD provides a detailed blueprint for
  apparel manufacturing, ensuring flawless execution of intricate construction
  elements like integrated bra strap details, metal rings, custom tie-ups, and
  strategic cut-outs.

The documentation captures precise material specifications, integrating versatile fabrics such as Poly Lycra S/J, Stretchable Lycra Net, and structured 230 GSM Poly Ponte Roma. To guarantee accurate bulk production, the linesheets include exact Pantone (TPG) colorway matching across bold solid colors—such as Hot Pink (17-1937 TPG) and Greenlake (17-5528 TPG).
